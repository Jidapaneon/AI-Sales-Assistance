# Executive Summary

## Business Context

B2B sales teams lose productive selling time when reps manually summarize meetings, update CRM records inconsistently, and chase opportunities without a clear prioritization model. Managers also struggle to see which deals need attention, which follow-ups are overdue, and where the pipeline is at risk.

## Proposed Solution

The AI Sales Assistant is a sales copilot that combines CRM data, meeting notes, lead scoring, and AI-generated follow-up support in a KPI dashboard. It helps reps spend less time on administration and helps managers focus coaching on the deals most likely to move revenue.

## Target Users

Sales representatives use the assistant to summarize meetings, clean CRM notes, draft follow-up emails, and identify next best actions. Sales managers use the dashboard to monitor conversion, response time, pipeline risk, and AI adoption. Sales operations and revenue leadership use KPI trends to measure productivity and commercial impact.

## Expected Impact

The project targets measurable improvements in sales productivity: faster response times, more consistent follow-up, fewer stale opportunities, reduced administrative hours, and higher conversion rates. The first version uses simple scoring and optional LLM-based generation so the value can be demonstrated quickly without heavy system integration.

## Success Measures

Primary KPIs are lead conversion rate, average response time, hours spent on admin work, pipeline health, and AI usage. A pilot would compare baseline CRM performance against AI-assisted workflows over a defined sales cycle.
