# Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Needs | Pain Today | AI Sales Assistant Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales Representative | Spend more time selling and less time documenting. | Manual meeting summaries, inconsistent follow-up, unclear lead priority. | Auto summaries, email drafts, prioritized next actions. |
| Sales Manager | Coach reps and protect pipeline quality. | Limited visibility into stale deals and inconsistent CRM notes. | Risk dashboard, rep-level activity insights, pipeline health metrics. |
| Sales Operations | Improve CRM hygiene and reporting accuracy. | Missing fields, messy notes, uneven process adoption. | Note cleanup, structured fields, AI usage tracking. |
| Revenue Leadership | Improve conversion and forecast confidence. | Hard to measure productivity improvements. | KPI baseline, conversion trend, time saved estimate. |
| Customer / Prospect | Receive relevant and timely follow-up. | Delayed responses or generic communication. | Faster, more contextual follow-up reviewed by reps. |
| Legal / Compliance | Protect customer data and control AI risk. | Unclear AI governance and data use boundaries. | Human review, audit trail, sensitive data controls. |
