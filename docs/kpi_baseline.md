# KPI Baseline

The first version uses illustrative baseline values. Replace them with CRM exports during a real pilot.

| KPI | Baseline | AI-Assisted Target | Measurement Method |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Average response time | 18 hours | 8 hours | Time from meeting end to follow-up email. |
| Lead conversion rate | 22% | 28% | Won opportunities divided by closed opportunities. |
| Admin hours per rep per week | 8 hours | 4.5 hours | Rep time survey and CRM activity logs. |
| Stale opportunities | 32% | 18% | Opportunities with no activity in more than 14 days. |
| CRM note quality | 60% complete | 85% complete | Required fields and manager quality review. |
| AI usage rate | 0% | 70% | Share of opportunities using at least one AI feature. |

## KPI Logic

- Time saved = baseline admin hours minus AI-assisted admin hours.
- Pipeline health = weighted score from stage, probability, days since last activity, deal value, and risk flags.
- Conversion lift = AI-assisted conversion rate minus baseline conversion rate.
